Carbonara Recipe

Description

Carbonara is a classic Italian pasta dish made with eggs, cheese, pancetta, and black pepper.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti
  • Cheddar
  • Eggs
  • Bacon or Mushroom
  • Black Pepper
  • Garlic Paste

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. Meanwhile, grate the cheese and whisk with the eggs.
  3. In a pan, cook the bacon or mushroom with the garlic paste until crispy or softened.
  4. Drain the spaghetti, then add to the pan for a minute and mix well with plenty of black pepper.
  5. Take the pan off the heat, immediately pour in the egg and cheese mixture and stir vigorously until well mixed.
  6. Serve with additional black pepper as desired.

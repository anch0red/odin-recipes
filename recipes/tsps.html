Tuna Sweetcorn Pasta Salad Recipe

Description

Tuna Sweetcorn pasta salad is an easy, large quantity, lunch meal to cover you for about a week in the quantities described.

Ingredients

  • 1kg Fusili
  • 2 Tins 380g Tuna
  • 2 Tins Sweetcorn
  • 2 Packets Baby Plum Tomato
  • 2 Cucumbers
  • Mayo
  • Salad Cream

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Meanwhile, chop the cucumbers into halfed slices and the tomatoes into halves.
  3. Drain the tuna and the sweetcorn, flake the tuna with a fork.
  4. Drain the pasta and flush with cold water until chill.
  5. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl with large quantities of mayo and a small amount of salad cream.
  6. Mix well and serve.

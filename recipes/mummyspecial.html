Mummy Special

Description

This is a special recipe that I used to make that I learnt either from my mum or for my mum. It's basically a dessert recipe, incredibly sweet, fatty, and almost certainly unhealthy in any quantity.

Ingredients

  • Weetabix
  • Butter
  • Brown Sugar

Instructions

  1. Get however many weetabix you want out and put them on a plate.
  2. Spread the butter over each weetabix.
  3. Pour the brown sugar over the top as a coating.
  4. Eat!
